Kim's newest book is a colorful, rhyming adventure featuring Gritty,
Grandpa Goat, and Gritty's cousin Gibby. As they work together to build a
tree house, Gibby learns an important lesson about perseverance: achieving
a goal often takes patience, problem-solving, and the willingness to keep
trying.

Filled with humor, heart, and an inspiring message about perseverance, this
uplifting story encourages young readers to believe in themselves, embrace
mistakes, and remember that any little goat can grow up with grit!
