Growing with Gritty

Build Grit. Grow Strong. Never Quit!

One page to react to before any real page is built: the colors, the lettering, and the feel of the site's main pieces — using Kim's own hand-drawn Gritty and a real book cover. Nothing here is final; say what feels right and what doesn't.

02

The lettering

Fredoka — headings & buttons

Any little goat can grow up with grit!

Fredoka medium — section titles

🌱 Gritty's Calm Corner

Nunito — reading text

Kim's books are designed to be fun, engaging, and meaningful for children, while giving parents and teachers opportunities to talk about important life lessons — grit, kindness, confidence, and a positive mindset.

Patrick Hand — Gritty's speech bubbles only

"I have grit! I don't quit!"

Why these three

  • Fredoka is rounded and friendly — storybook lettering that still reads clearly at any size.
  • Nunito keeps paragraphs easy for grown-ups and early readers alike; generous sizes throughout.
  • Patrick Hand feels like neat teacher handwriting — and it belongs to Gritty's voice alone. One font, one job, so the pages never turn into a scrapbook.
  • Buttons and tap targets are big (48px+) — little fingers, and grandparents' too.
