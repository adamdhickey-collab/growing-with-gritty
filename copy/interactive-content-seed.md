## Gritty's voice (the guide)

Intro speech bubble:

> "Hi, friend! I'm Gritty. I don't always get things right the first time.
> But that's okay! I keep trying, learn from my mistakes, and never give up."

Recurring motto: **"I have grit! I don't quit!"**
Recurring frame: **"What would Gritty do?"**
Homepage reminder: *"Remember: little by little, you grow BIG!"*

## What is grit? (picture row — Meet Gritty)

🧩 Trying something hard · 🧗 Keeping going · 💡 Trying a new idea ·
🤝 Asking for help · 🔄 Trying again · ❤️ Believing in yourself

## Scenarios (Grit Zone) — seed entry #1

- **Situation:** Gritty is trying to build a tower, but it keeps falling
  down. What should he do?
- **Choices:** A. Give up. · B. Get mad and walk away. ·
  C. Take a breath and try a new way. ⭐
- **Gritty's cheer:** "That's grit! You didn't give up — you tried a new
  strategy!"

Target: 5–10 scenarios for launch (Kim writes the rest — tower, tricky word,
lost game, new kid, tangled shoelaces…).

## Mistakes (The Mistake Maker) — seed entry #1

- **Oops:** "Gritty tried to draw a square… but it looks more like a potato!"
- **Options:** Try Again · Learn From It · Ask for Help · Take a Break
- **Gritty says:** "Mistakes don't mean I'm bad at something. Mistakes help
  my brain learn!"
- **Big button:** 🔄 TURN MY "OOPS!" INTO "I LEARNED!"

## YET sentences (The Power of YET)

- I can't tie my shoes… **YET!**
- I can't read this word… **YET!**
- I can't ride my bike… **YET!**
- I can't solve this problem… **YET!**
- I can't make a new friend… **YET!**

## Kindness challenges (Kindness Corner)

- Give someone a compliment.
- Help someone without being asked.
- Invite someone to play.
- Say thank you to someone who helped you.
- Notice someone who might need a friend.

## Feelings + calm strategies (Calm Corner)

Feelings: 😡 Angry · 😢 Sad · 😟 Worried · 😩 Frustrated · 😴 Tired · 😬 Nervous

Gritty: "Big feelings happen to everyone. Let's help your body feel calm."

Strategies:
- 🌬️ Take 5 slow breaths
- 🌸 Smell the flower, blow out the candle
- 🖐️ 5-4-3-2-1 Senses
- 🧘 Stretch with Gritty
- 💧 Take a drink of water
- 🚶 Take a movement break
- 💭 Use a kind thought
- 🗣️ Talk to a trusted grown-up
