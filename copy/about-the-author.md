## About the Author

Kim Rekowski is a dedicated elementary teacher, children's book author, and
illustrator who is passionate about helping children build confidence,
resilience, and a positive mindset.

Inspired by her experiences in the classroom, Kim created Gritty the Goat, a
lovable character who helps children discover what it means to have grit —
the courage to keep trying, even when things get difficult.

## Meet Gritty the Goat

In *Gritty the Goat*, readers ages 4–10 join Gritty on his first day of
school. Nervous and unsure of what to expect, Gritty receives some wise
advice from Grandpa Goat:

> "I have grit! I don't quit!"

Throughout his school day, Gritty learns that challenging moments can be
opportunities to grow. His story reminds children that they can overcome
difficult situations, learn from their experiences, and gain confidence
along the way.

## Growing with Gritty (the series)

*Gritty Grows a Garden*, the second book in the Gritty the Goat series,
follows Gritty as he works with Grandpa Goat to grow a garden. Along the
way, he discovers that gardens — and healthy habits — take patience, care,
and consistent effort to grow. By the end of summer, Gritty discovers a
special surprise!

The third book, *Gabby the Goat's Best Friend*, explores the joys and
challenges of friendship. Gabby helps young readers discover how to navigate
friendships, appreciate the people who care about them, and recognize the
importance of kindness and self-worth.

Kim's newest book, *Gritty Builds a Tree House*, is a colorful, rhyming
adventure featuring Gritty, Grandpa Goat, and Gritty's cousin Gibby. As they
work together to build a tree house, Gibby learns an important lesson about
perseverance: achieving a goal often takes patience, problem-solving, and
the willingness to keep trying. Filled with humor, heart, and an inspiring
message about perseverance, this uplifting story encourages young readers to
believe in themselves, embrace mistakes, and remember that any little goat
can grow up with grit!

## Growing Strong Minds and Hearts

Kim's books are designed to be fun, engaging, and meaningful for children,
while providing parents and teachers with opportunities to talk about
important life lessons.

Through Gritty and his friends, young readers learn about grit,
perseverance, friendship, healthy habits, confidence, kindness, and having a
positive mindset.

Kim hopes her stories inspire every child to believe in themselves, embrace
challenges, and remember:

> "I have grit! I don't quit!"

Keep growing. Keep learning. Keep trying.
