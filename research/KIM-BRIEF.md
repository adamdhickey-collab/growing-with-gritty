# What we learned from the best kids' book sites (for Kim)

Before designing the second version of your site's look, we studied about
twenty of the best comparable sites — character brands like Bluey, the
Gruffalo, and Mo Willems' Pigeon; author sites at your scale; and the big
kids' education brands like PBS Kids and GoNoodle. Here's what we're
borrowing, in plain language:

**What the best sites do that we'll do too**

- **The menu speaks kid:** Read · Play · Make — and one "Grown-Ups" corner
  where all the buying, teacher guides, and email stuff lives. Kids never
  see a buy button; parents instantly see the site is safe.
- **Every activity is named after your stories.** Not "coloring page" but
  "Gritty's Tower Challenge." The best sites (Roald Dahl's especially) do
  this so every free download reminds families of a specific book.
- **Your drawing hand is the star.** Mo Willems teaches kids to draw the
  Pigeon; Eric Carle explains his tissue-paper collage. A "Learn to draw
  Gritty with Kim" page would be the single most *you* thing on the site —
  and teachers love it.
- **Every kid activity comes with a grown-up half.** Like Storyline Online
  pairs each story with a teacher guide and a parent guide, each Gritty
  activity gets a matching "talk about it together" note.
- **Nothing to lose, things to collect.** The most-loved kids' brands (Toca
  Boca, Sago Mini) have no points, timers, or failing — kids collect
  things and characters celebrate them. So: stickers Gritty "leaves" for
  kids, saved only on their own device.

**One real opening:** Character Lab — Angela Duckworth's grit organization,
the biggest grit resource for parents and teachers — shut down in 2024.
Families searching for grit help have lost their main hub. Gritty can be
what they find.

**What we'd ask of you (only if you like this direction):** 3–4 new little
drawings of Gritty — waving, cheering, thinking, peeking around a corner —
so he can react to kids on the site. Same goat, just new poses.

Nothing here is decided. Version 2 of the style page will show all of this
next to Version 1, and you pick — or mix.
